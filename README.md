## Key Insights

* **Women have a higher purchase rate than men**, indicating that women are the major customer segment.
* **Amazon and Myntra generate the highest sales**, making them the most important sales channels.
* **Adult and Teenager women contribute significantly to sales**, showing strong demand from these customer groups.
* **Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, and Telangana** are among the top-performing states in terms of sales.

## Conclusion

Based on the analysis, our primary target customers should be **Adult and Teenager women**, as they contribute significantly to overall sales. We should focus on **Western and minimal, easy-to-wear clothing** that matches their preferences.
We should also focus our sales and marketing efforts on **Amazon, Myntra, and Flipkart**, as these channels show strong sales performance.
